    The service

    Teara is a personal trip-planning app for iPhone. It is provided as is and is still evolving. Some features are offered as a beta, including through TestFlight, and features may change or be removed.

    Check your travel details

    Teara extracts booking details automatically and can make mistakes. Before you travel, check flight times, addresses, dates and booking references against the original confirmation from the airline, hotel or booking site. That confirmation is the source of truth.

    Acceptable use

    Don't misuse your Teara forwarding address or shared trips. That includes sending spam, sharing unlawful content, and adding or forwarding other people's information without their permission.

    Connected accounts

    Teara uses connected accounts, such as Gmail and iCloud, only as described in the privacy policy. You can disconnect them at any time.

    Shared trips

    When you share a trip, the people you invite can see its details. Share a trip only with people you want to see those details.

    Limitation of liability

    To the extent the law allows, Teara is not liable for indirect or consequential losses, such as missed flights, bookings or connections, that result from using the app or relying on the information it shows.

    Suspension

    Teara may suspend or close accounts that abuse the service, including the forwarding address or shared trips.
