Privacy

Clear about your data.

Teara keeps only the information needed to build your trip and provide the services you choose to use. This page explains what is handled, why it is needed, and where it goes.

Last updated October 18, 2026

The Teara app

Information you provide

When you connect forwarded bookings, Teara uses Sign in with Apple to create your forwarding account. The relay receives only messages sent to your personal Teara forwarding address. It does not read or scan your inbox.

The relay may temporarily hold the forwarded message subject, body text, and supported PDF attachments so your device can retrieve them. Teara processes the booking details on your device and lets you review them before adding anything to a trip.

Account and notification data

Teara stores the identifier supplied by Sign in with Apple, the name you choose to share, your personal forwarding address, authentication tokens, and an Apple Push Notification service device token when notifications are enabled. Booking alerts contain no booking details.

Your trips

Your trips and trip building blocks are stored on your device and sync through the private iCloud account signed into that device. If you share a trip, its details are stored on Teara's server so the people you invite can see them. Teara does not use trip data for advertising and does not sell it.

Retention

Forwarded messages are marked as received after your device retrieves them. Retrieved messages are deleted from the relay after 7 days. Messages that have not been retrieved are deleted after 30 days. You can delete your forwarding account in the app. Your trips remain on your device and in your private iCloud account unless you delete them there.

Gmail import (optional)

If you connect your Gmail account, Teara asks Google for read-only access to your email (the gmail.readonly permission). Teara can't send, delete or change your email.

What Teara reads

Teara searches your mailbox only for travel confirmations, such as flights, hotels, car rentals, tours and booking sites, from the period you choose (1 to 12 months back), and after that only new mail. Matching emails and their attachments are downloaded to your iPhone.

Where it's processed

Emails are read on your iPhone, by Teara's built-in rules and Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence). Email content is never sent to Teara's servers or to any other AI service. Your Google sign-in token is kept in your iPhone's Keychain.

What's kept

Emails that aren't travel bookings are skipped, and Teara keeps only their message IDs so it doesn't download them again. Booking details you add to a trip, such as flight numbers, times, places and booking references, become part of that trip. Trips are stored on your iPhone and synced through your own iCloud account. If you share a trip, its details are stored on Teara's server so the people you invite can see them. The original email is never uploaded.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect Gmail at any time in Teara under Account → Gmail. This revokes Teara's access with Google and deletes the downloaded emails from your iPhone. Bookings you already added to trips stay until you delete them. You can also remove Teara's access from your Google Account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What Teara never does

Teara doesn't use your email for advertising, doesn't sell or share it with third parties, doesn't use it to train AI models, and no person at Teara reads it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Teara's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Service providers

Teara relies on Apple for Sign in with Apple, iCloud, and push notifications, on Cloudflare for the booking relay and limited operational storage, and, if you connect Gmail, on Google for sign-in and read-only access to your mailbox.

Each provider handles information under its own terms and privacy policy. Teara shares only the information needed to provide the requested function.

LinkedIn authorization

Teara may use LinkedIn's authorized tools to manage its official company page. Access is granted through LinkedIn and limited to the permissions needed for approved page management. It can be revoked through LinkedIn at any time.

Tracking and advertising

Teara does not track people across apps or websites. Teara does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Security

Teara uses encrypted connections for data in transit and provider security controls for stored data. Access credentials are kept outside public source code. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but Teara limits collection and access to what each feature needs.

Your choices

  • You choose which confirmations to forward to Teara.
  • You review imported booking details before adding them to a trip.
  • You can disable notifications in system settings.
  • You can disconnect and delete your Teara forwarding account in the app.
  • You can disconnect Gmail in the app under Account → Gmail, or from your Google Account.
  • You can revoke a connected LinkedIn authorization through LinkedIn.

Changes and questions

This notice may change as Teara grows. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes materially.
