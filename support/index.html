Support

Have a question, found a bug, or have an idea for Teara? Here's how to reach us.

On this page

    Get in touch

    Email supportsupport@teara.io, for help, bugs and feedback. Join the betaTry future features on TestFlight and send feedback from there.

    Report an issue

    To help us understand what happened, include:

    • Your device model and iOS version.
    • Your Teara version and build number. Both are shown in the app.
    • What you were trying to do and what happened instead.
    • The steps to reproduce the issue and a screenshot, if helpful.

    Hide personal details and booking references in screenshots before sharing them.

    Common questions

    Does Teara read my email?

    Only if you connect Gmail, and only travel confirmations. They're read on your iPhone; the email content never goes to Teara's servers. You can disconnect anytime in Account → Gmail. How Teara handles your email

    Is my trip information private?

    Booking emails are processed on your device, and your trip data syncs through your private iCloud account. If you share a trip, its details are stored on Teara's server so the people you invite can see them.

    Does booking import work on every supported device?

    Teara processes booking details directly on your device. The range of confirmations it can understand may vary by device, and support will continue to expand with future updates.

    Is Teara still evolving?

    Yes. Teara is live on the App Store and continues to grow. The TestFlight program remains open so you can test future features and share feedback before they reach the App Store.

    Why was Teara created?

    Teara began as a personal project for a trip to New Zealand. A shared document could hold the details, but it felt too static for planning and experiencing the trip together. Teara was created as one focused place for bookings and daily plans, without the clutter of a general-purpose tool.

    More basics are in the FAQ on the home page.
