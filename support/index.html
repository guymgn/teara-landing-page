Report an issue

To help us understand what happened, include:

  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • Your Teara version and build number — both are available in the app.
  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead.
  • The steps to reproduce the issue and a screenshot, if helpful.

Please hide personal details and booking references in screenshots before sharing them.
