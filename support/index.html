More questions

Why was Teara created?

Teara began as a personal project for a trip to New Zealand. A shared document could hold the details, but it felt too static for planning and experiencing the trip together. Teara was created as one focused place for bookings and daily plans, without the clutter of a general-purpose tool.

Is my trip information private?

Booking emails are processed on your device. Your trip data syncs through your private iCloud account, and Teara does not read your inbox.

Does booking import work on every supported device?

Teara processes booking details directly on your device. The range of confirmations it can understand may vary by device, and support will continue to expand with future updates.

Is Teara still evolving?

Yes. Teara is live on the App Store and continues to grow. The TestFlight program remains open so you can test future features and share feedback before they reach the App Store.
