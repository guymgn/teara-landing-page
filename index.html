Shared trips

Invite your people along.

Invite the people traveling with you to share your trip. Keep your bookings and plans together in one shared itinerary, so everyone knows what’s next.

Trip checklist

Get ready before you fly.

Teara suggests what to check before you go, like passport validity, visas, and travel insurance for the countries on your trip. Tick items off as you finish them, and keep your packing list in the same place.

    Good to know

    Questions about Teara.

    What is Teara?

    Teara is an iPhone app for organizing trips. It keeps booking details, destinations, reservations, and daily plans in one itinerary.

    How do booking emails become part of a trip?

    Forward a confirmation email to your personal Teara address. The app extracts the relevant details on your device. You can review and edit them before adding the booking to a trip.

    What can I add to Teara?

    You can add accommodation, vehicle rentals, flights and other transport, dining reservations, activities, adventures, and notes. Teara currently supports booking confirmation emails and PDF documents.

    How much does Teara cost?

    Teara is completely free to use.

    Where can I download Teara?

    Teara is live on the App Store. Download the app for the current release. To test future features before they are released, join the ongoing TestFlight program.
